Privacy Policy · DocuServi-PDF & Img maker

Effective date: 16 October 2026 · Last updated: 16 October 2026

The short version: DocuServi-PDF & Img maker does not collect your data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The photos, PDFs and pages you open are processed entirely on your own device and are never uploaded anywhere.

In one line: your files stay on your device, and nothing about you is collected, stored or shared by us.

1. Who we are

DocuServi-PDF & Img maker (the “App”) is an offline document and image utility designed and developed by Aman Katiyar (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”). This policy explains what the App does with information when you install and use it. You can reach us at docuservi940@gmail.com.

2. What the App does with your files

The App has three tools:

  • Images → PDF — turns photos and scans into a PDF.
  • PDF → Images — renders the pages of a PDF into images.
  • Resize Photos — resizes photos to a size you choose.

Every one of these operations runs locally, inside the App, on your device. The files you select are read from your device, processed in memory, and written back to your device’s Downloads folder (or the location you choose) when you tap save. They are never transmitted to us or to anyone else. We have no server that receives them, and no ability to see them.

3. What we do not collect

We do not collect, request, store or share any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any contact details
  • Your photos, images, PDFs or any file you process
  • Your location
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs or account identifiers
  • Usage statistics, crash reports or analytics of any kind
  • Any payment or financial information

The App has no user accounts and no login. We cannot identify you, and we do not try to.

4. Permissions the App asks for

The Android build of the App declares no permissions at all — not even the INTERNET permission. It therefore cannot reach the network, your camera, your microphone, your location, your contacts or your accounts, because it never asks for the ability to.

The only thing the App does outside itself is write the file you asked it to create (a PDF, a PNG or JPEG image, or a ZIP) into your device’s public Downloads folder so that you can find it. It reads nothing except the specific files you choose in the system file picker, and it never copies, uploads or shares them.

5. The App does not use the internet

Earlier versions of the App loaded their typeface (IBM Plex) from Google’s font service. That is no longer the case: the typefaces are now bundled inside the App itself, so the App makes no network requests of any kind.

On Android the App does not even request the INTERNET permission, which means the operating system will not allow it to reach the network at all. Every feature works with your device in aeroplane mode.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

7. Data retention and deletion

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain, sell or delete. Files you create are saved on your own device and remain entirely under your control — delete them from your Downloads folder whenever you like. Uninstalling the App removes the App and its local settings; it does not touch the files you saved.

8. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, trade or share personal information with anyone, because we do not have any to share.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the EU/UK GDPR — for example, the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete. If you believe otherwise, write to us at docuservi940@gmail.com and we will look into it.

11. Security

Because your files never leave your device, there is no transmission for anyone to intercept and no server of ours that could be breached. The App declares no permissions, opens no network ports and makes no network requests.
